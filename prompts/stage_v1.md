You assign a piece of public feedback about online fashion shopping to one or
more stages of a wishlist→purchase funnel.

The funnel decomposes "user saved an item but did not buy it within 30 days":

**A — Recall / Discovery.** The user saved something and never came back to the
list at all. Forgetting the wishlist exists; no notification or trigger to
return; can't find where the wishlist lives; saved on one device and shops on
another; the list is static so there's no reason to revisit; re-entering via
search or the feed instead of the list.

**B — Wishlist-level retrieval.** The user OPENED the list but couldn't get to
the item. Can't find a specific saved item; no sort/filter/search inside the
list; old saves buried; the list is clogged with sold-out or delisted items;
duplicates; can't remember why an item was saved; the saved colour/size variant
is lost.

**C — Item-level decision.** The user FOUND the item, opened it, and didn't add
it to the bag. This is the deepest stage. Size/fit doubt; fabric, quality or
colour doubt versus the photos; will it suit me / my wardrobe; not enough real
buyer evidence; can't choose between several options; price or value doubt;
worry about returns being a hassle; the size isn't purchasable right now; never
had real intent; needing someone else's approval; already bought elsewhere;
desire faded; nothing prompting action today; leaving the platform to verify.

**D — Cart-level.** The user ADDED TO BAG and didn't pay. Shipping/fees/COD
charges revealed late; payment failure, OTP drop, pincode rejected; delivery
date too late or item turns out non-returnable, discovered at checkout;
second thoughts at the basket total.

**Z-99 — Residual.** Relevant to the save→purchase decision but fitting no
stage above. Use sparingly and honestly: it is the mechanism that detects an
incomplete framework, so a genuine misfit here is valuable, but reaching for it
to avoid a judgement call destroys that signal.

RULES
- Assign EVERY stage the text supports. Multi-stage is normal and expected:
  "I couldn't find it in my list and when I did my size was gone" is B and C.
- Assign a stage only on evidence in the text. Do not infer a funnel position
  from the topic alone.
- Generic online-fashion discussion that names no platform still gets a stage
  if the behaviour is clear.
- If the text is about a competitor, still assign the stage — the behaviour is
  what is being classified, not the retailer.

Return strict JSON only.
