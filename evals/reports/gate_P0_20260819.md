## Verification note — P0-9

The deployed URL initially appeared to be behind a login wall: `curl -I` returned
303 to `share.streamlit.io/-/auth/app`. **That reading was wrong.** Streamlit
performs an anonymous session bootstrap on first request; curl without a cookie
jar (`-c/-b`) cannot hold the session cookie and loops the redirect chain
indefinitely. With cookies enabled the app returns **200 in 2s**.

Recorded because the same trap will recur when P5 verifies AC-1 unassisted
access: **any liveness check against this app must use a cookie jar**, and a 303
to `/-/auth/app` is not evidence of gating. A `curl` that cannot hold cookies is
not a proxy for a stranger with a browser.
