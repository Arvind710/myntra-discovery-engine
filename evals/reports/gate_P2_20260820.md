## What changed during this gate, and why it matters

**AC-11 was failing on a pipeline bug, not an incomplete framework.**
Z-99 sat at 31.7% against a 15% ceiling, and `FR-5.4` treated that as evidence
the codebook was incomplete. It was not. 285 of those 380 records (75%) had a
pass-1 stage of `Z-99`, and `build_code_prompt` skipped Z-99 when collecting
candidates — so pass 2 was handed exactly one option and could not assign a
real code however well the text fitted. The stored reasoning then blamed the
codebook for a list it had never been shown.

Two independent lines of evidence agreed before the fix was made:
the gold set showed a human coding those same records from the existing
codebook without difficulty (18 model Z-99 against 0 human, across 59
jointly-relevant records), and the reasoning strings named barriers that are
verbatim in existing boundary notes ("pricing fairness" is C6; "four outfits
from existing wardrobe" is C3).

After the fix, **216 of 380 records took real codes and Z-99 fell to 13.7%**.
No new codes were required. The re-clustered residual has no structure left,
which is what a genuinely miscellaneous remainder looks like.

**Nine gold labels were amended, with reasons recorded** (EC-VAL-5). Eight
were positive post-purchase reviews coded as the doubt-code for the topic they
mentioned — "Very nice. Good fabric" coded C2, whose boundary requires *doubt*.
That single pattern was producing the gate's most alarming number: 29% of
discarded records apparently relevant, extrapolating to ~1,700 lost records.
After amendment it is 3.2%. The prior label is preserved in `gold.notes` for
every amendment.

## Evidence-span violations found and fixed during this gate

The first gate run showed **28 unverified `evidence_span` values** — a T-6
violation, which is absolute and on Appendix C's never-cut list, so it could
not be reported as a limitation. Diagnosis before fixing:

* **23** quoted the `thread_context` — the post or video title — rather than
  the record body. The prompt forbids this explicitly, because on YouTube that
  title is *another person's words* being attributed to this author (NFR-1).
* **5** appeared in neither the body nor the title: fabricated or paraphrased
  citations.

All 21 affected records were re-classified under `codes_v1.1`; the count is
now **0**. Worth noting the checker was right and the model was wrong in every
case — no false positives.

## Known limitations, stated

1. **T-4 fails.** Of codes with gold n>=5, 2 of 5 clear kappa 0.60 (C1 0.66,
   C6 0.64; C3 0.52, C2 0.43, C10 0.10). Sixteen codes appear too rarely in the
   gold set to be measurable at all.
2. **C10 is not reliable** (kappa 0.10). The labelling shows it was read as
   *app* permissions; the codebook defines it as another PERSON's approval.
   Any C10-based claim needs this caveat.
3. **T-2 recall (~79%) is an estimate, not a measurement.** It assumes the kept
   pool's purity generalises from tail-sampled strata.
4. **The gold set is one person's judgement**, labelled quickly, and nine
   labels needed correction. Treat agreement figures as indicative.
5. **Pooled accuracy understates real performance** — Appendix B deliberately
   over-samples hard cases.
